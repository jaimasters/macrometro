# Land acquisition
- Building cost = base(track class, length) + land(cells crossed).
- Greenfield cell: flat cheap rate. Developed cell: compulsory acquisition = k_density * land_value * (1 + disruption), land_value rises with A_i.
- Reservation: player may cheaply reserve a corridor on greenfield before growth; reserved cells grow nothing. Rewards foresight.
- Cost unit depends on economy proposal (proposals/economy.md): money or political capital.
- Demolition displaces pop -> temporary growth penalty in zone.
