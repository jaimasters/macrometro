# Game's own role in the city (proposal)
STATUS: A (modified) CHOSEN, see DECISIONS D-7.
- City is an autonomous agent: road agency builds roads where unserved demand persists (transit share < s_min for T ticks in a zone).
- Roads lower car cost -> car share rises -> sprawl (low-density growth along roads) -> longer trips -> transit harder. This IS the LA death spiral; reversible only by player transit + costly acquisition of road corridors.
- Knob: road-agency aggressiveness rises with cumulative failure (difficulty drift).
- Alternative B: city never builds; failure only via crowding/pop stall. Weaker narrative.
- Recommendation: A, with visible "road agency plans" shown 1 cycle ahead so player can pre-empt.
