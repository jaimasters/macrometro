# Economy / success proposals (Jai to choose)
STATUS: P1 (modified) CHOSEN, see DECISIONS D-6, D-8.
Context: agency is non-profit; Jai dislikes money as primary; wants diverse metrics incl. efficiency.
## P1 Political capital + scorecard (RECOMMENDED)
- Currency = mandate (political capital), earned per tick from a scorecard: accessibility gain, mean trip time, transit share, crowding, emissions, coverage equity. Spent on construction/acquisition.
- Efficiency metric = person-km delivered per track-km (or per mandate spent).
- + Ties success directly to good transit; non-profit framing natural; diverse metrics.
- - Abstract; needs tuning so one metric doesn't dominate.
## P2 Budget grant from ridership + subsidy
- Money exists; government grant scales with ridership & farebox recovery.
- + Familiar, easy to balance. - Money-centric, the thing Jai wants to avoid; irrelevant at scale.
## P3 No currency; capacity-limited construction crews
- Build speed limited by crews; crews grow with city satisfaction. Score = scorecard only.
- + Simplest, no economy. - Weak trade-offs; acquisition vs greenfield becomes time cost only.
Recommendation: P1, with greenfield/acquisition priced in mandate (land-acquisition.md).
