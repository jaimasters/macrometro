- D-1 | 2026-10-12 | Cargo workspace, one crate per feature, each exposes one Bevy Plugin | isolation for agents | monolith.
- D-2 | 2026-10-12 | Zone flows authoritative; agents visual-only | cost scales with zones not pop | agent-based sim.

- D-4 | 2026-10-12 | Visual style abstract 3D diagram (Jai) | also caps material count -> fewer pipeline hitches.
- D-5 | 2026-10-12 | Endless sandbox; city move = reset; difficulty drift to LA sprawl (Jai).
- D-6 | 2026-10-12 | Economy = P1 modified (Jai): city allocates agency a per-period budget (mandate) sized by scorecard + political sentiment; budgets carry soft conditions ("spend >=X% on Y"); missing them costs political standing, never blocks. Car-dependent cities show favouritism: road-adjacent earmarks, pet projects, inefficient forced spend.
- D-7 | 2026-10-12 | City role = A modified (Jai): city reserves part of its budget for its own road building when it judges people underserved; reserve competes with agency allocation. UI surfaces it subtly: faint planned-road ghosts + quiet budget-breakdown line.
- D-8 | 2026-10-12 | Multiplayer-ready (Jai): agency is an entity keyed by AgencyId (no singleton); city budget split per agency weighted by agency performance (favouritism lives in the weight). Sim stays deterministic + input-driven (commands carry AgencyId) for future lockstep/server-authoritative play. No netcode now.
- D-9 | 2026-10-12 | PROVISIONAL defaults: scorecard weights equal across {accessibility, trip time, transit share, crowding, emissions, coverage equity}; all shown, top-3 deltas highlighted. Old cities: observe + light edits (line policies only), no new construction. Rail mismatch penalty: up to -30% effective speed/capacity, scaled by stop-spacing and alignment mismatch.
